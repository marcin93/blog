## What you get (3 to 5 working days)

1. **Inventory and architecture map.** Versions, integrations, data flows, hosting, HA status.
2. **Risk register.** Security, availability, EOL and upgrade, license, compliance, bus factor. Rated and prioritized.
3. **Stability and performance findings.** Backup and restore validity, monitoring gaps, common failure modes.
4. **Migration readiness verdict.** Server to Data Center to Cloud, and consolidation feasibility, with an effort estimate.
5. **Prioritized 90 day remediation roadmap.** Now, next, later, with effort and impact.
6. **60 minute findings walkthrough** with your team.

**Deliverable:** a written report (PDF) plus a roadmap. No vendor lock in, no sales pitch. Actionable as is, by your team or mine.

**Format:** remote or on site in the Stuttgart region. Mostly async, with minimal demand on your team. One kickoff call, read only access, one closing call.
