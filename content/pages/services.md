## ALM Toolchain Health-Check

**A fixed-scope assessment of your engineering ALM/SDLC platform - risks, stability, upgrade path, and migration readiness.**

For R&D organizations running **Codebeamer, Atlassian (Jira / Confluence / Bitbucket), Teamwork Cloud / MagicDraw, Sparx EA** in regulated or post-merger environments.

---

### Why now

- Atlassian Server is end-of-life and Data Center licensing is shifting - unplanned migration risk.
- Post-M&A toolchains accumulate hidden config drift, broken integrations, and audit gaps.
- Single-person tool ownership = bus-factor and compliance exposure (Automotive SPICE / ISO 26262).

---

### What you get (3–5 working days)

1. **Inventory & architecture map** - versions, integrations, data flows, hosting, HA status.
2. **Risk register** - security, availability, EOL/upgrade, license, compliance, bus-factor - rated and prioritized.
3. **Stability & performance findings** - backup/restore validity, monitoring gaps, common failure modes.
4. **Migration readiness verdict** - Server→DC→Cloud / consolidation feasibility, effort estimate.
5. **Prioritized 90-day remediation roadmap** - Now / Next / Later, with effort and impact.
6. **60-minute findings walkthrough** with your team.

**Deliverable:** written report (PDF) + roadmap. No vendor lock-in, no sales pitch - actionable as-is, by your team or mine.

**Format:** Remote or on-site (Stuttgart region). Mostly async - minimal demand on your team's time (one kickoff call, read-access, one closing call).
